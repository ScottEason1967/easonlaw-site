Services

Operating model & transformation

How an organisation actually works underneath the strategy, so that structure, roles and decisions line up with where the business is heading.

What we do

We help boards and leadership teams design the operating model that sits beneath the strategy, and then support the change so it actually takes hold rather than fading once the project ends. The work is practical and grounded in how the organisation really runs day to day.

  • Structure, roles and accountabilities, and who owns which outcomes
  • Decision rights and governance, so the right decisions are made at the right level
  • How functions join up, and where the handoffs and gaps currently cost you
  • The processes and ways of working that turn strategy into delivery
  • A practical plan for the change, so it sticks beyond the launch

Where it helps

Some of the situations we are most often asked about:

  • Scaling, where the way you worked at a smaller size no longer fits
  • Bringing two organisations or teams together after a merger or acquisition
  • A strategy that looks right on paper but is not translating into delivery
  • Unclear accountabilities, slow decisions or persistent governance gaps
  • Standing up a new function or capability from scratch

Make the strategy real

If structure and strategy have drifted apart, we can help you close the gap. Tell us where it feels stuck and we will tell you how we would approach it.
