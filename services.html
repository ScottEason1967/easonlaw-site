Services

What we do.

Five areas where careful work early saves a great deal of difficulty later. Each one is shaped around what your business actually needs rather than a standard template.

01

Shareholder agreements & articles of association

The documents that decide how owners work together and what happens when circumstances change. We draft and review shareholder agreements and articles so that rights, controls, decision making and exit terms are clear and consistent, and so the company is set up the way the owners actually intend.

Read more → 02

Share reorganisations & Companies House

Restructuring share capital, creating new share classes, handling allotments and transfers, and preparing the board and shareholder paperwork that sits behind them. We take care of the Companies House filings so the changes are properly recorded and stand up to scrutiny later.

Read more → 03

Commercial contracts & NDAs

The agreements a business depends on day to day. From non disclosure agreements and supplier terms to share purchase agreements and bespoke commercial contracts, we review and draft with a clear eye on where the real risk sits and what is worth negotiating.

Read more → 04

Operating model & transformation

How an organisation works underneath the strategy. We help boards and leadership teams design the operating model, so that structure, roles, accountabilities and decision rights line up with where the business is heading and the change actually sticks.

Read more → 05

Statutory Registers

Every company is required by law to keep its statutory books, and most don't. Our online service builds the full set from the public record in about a minute, flags anything that doesn't add up, and keeps the books current for a simple monthly subscription.
