Services

Commercial contracts & NDAs

The agreements a business runs on, drafted and reviewed with a clear eye on the risk that actually matters and the points worth negotiating.

What we do

We draft, review and negotiate the contracts that sit at the centre of how a business operates. On a review, we focus on where the real exposure sits rather than marking up every clause, and we tell you plainly which points are worth pushing on and which are not.

  • Non disclosure agreements, one way and mutual
  • Terms and conditions of supply and purchase, services agreements and master services agreements
  • Statements of work, consultancy and contractor agreements
  • Distribution, agency and reseller arrangements
  • Share and asset purchase agreements, settlement agreements and variations

Where it helps

Some of the situations we are most often asked about:

  • Signing with a new customer or supplier and wanting the terms checked first
  • Protecting confidential information before opening discussions
  • Standardising your own contract templates so the business is consistent
  • Reviewing an incoming contract with one eye on liability, indemnities, IP and data
  • Buying or selling a business or a set of assets

Know where the risk really sits

Send us the agreement and a couple of lines on what you are trying to achieve, and we will come back with a clear view of the risk and the handful of points actually worth negotiating.
